PURPOSE
-------
All patient information is confidential. Staff may access only the information needed for their role.

ACCESS BY ROLE
--------------
- Administration (clerks): patient profiles, registration records, appointment registers, clerk work logs. No access to nursing, consultation, laboratory or medication records.
- Nursing: patient profiles, medical history summaries, nursing records, laboratory reports, medication records, referral records, discharge summaries and appointment registers. Consultation records are restricted to doctors and Medical Records.
- Doctors: all clinical records of patients, plus profiles and appointment registers.
- Laboratory: laboratory reports only (plus policies and the staff directory).
- Medical Records: all patient records for filing, summarising and quality control.
- Policies and the staff directory are open to all staff.

RULES
-----
- Staff must not discuss patients in public areas.
- Patient information is released to third parties only with the patient's written consent or as required by law.
- Every access to electronic records must be logged.
- Suspected breaches must be reported to the clinic manager within 24 hours.
