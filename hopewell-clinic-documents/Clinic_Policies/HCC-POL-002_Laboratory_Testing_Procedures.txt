SAMPLE COLLECTION
-----------------
- Confirm the patient's name and Patient ID against the request form.
- Label every sample at the bedside with Patient ID, date and time of collection.
- Record the technician ID of the person collecting the sample.

TESTING AND VERIFICATION
------------------------
- Routine full blood count results are verified within 6 hours of collection.
- Each result is compared with the reference range and flagged HIGH or LOW when outside it.
- A technician verifies the report and an authorised practitioner reviews it.

REPORTING
---------
- Laboratory reports state test results only. They are not diagnoses; interpretation is done by the treating practitioner.
- Tests that were not requested or not performed are reported as 'Not available'. No value may be estimated or invented.
- Critical values are telephoned to the treating practitioner immediately.
