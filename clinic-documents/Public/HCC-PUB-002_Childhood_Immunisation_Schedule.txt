This is the routine childhood schedule followed at the clinic. It follows the national
Expanded Programme on Immunisation (EPI). Always bring the Road-to-Health booklet.

AGE              VACCINES GIVEN
At birth         BCG (tuberculosis), OPV 0 (oral polio)
6 weeks          Pentavalent 1 (DTP-HepB-Hib), OPV 1, PCV 1 (pneumococcal), Rotavirus 1
10 weeks         Pentavalent 2, OPV 2, PCV 2, Rotavirus 2
14 weeks         Pentavalent 3, OPV 3, PCV 3, IPV (injectable polio)
9 months         Measles-Rubella 1 (MR1)
18 months        Measles-Rubella 2 (MR2), DTP booster
Girls 9-14 years HPV (cervical cancer prevention), 1 dose

MISSED A DOSE?
Come to any immunisation day. Missed doses are caught up; the schedule does not
restart. A mild fever or sore leg for 1-2 days after vaccination is normal.
