ABOUT THE CLINIC
Hopewell Community Clinic is a primary health care clinic serving Khomasdal,
Okahandja Park and Havana. About 1 900 patients are registered. The clinic has one
doctor (two days a week), two nurses and a clinic clerk.

OPENING HOURS
- Monday to Friday: 07:30 - 17:00 (last patient registered at 16:00)
- Saturday: 08:00 - 12:00 (immunisation and minor ailments only)
- Sunday and public holidays: closed
- Holiday closure: 24 December 2026 to 2 January 2027
For emergencies when the clinic is closed, call the emergency line
+264 61 555 0199 (fictional) or go to Katutura State Hospital or Windhoek Central
Hospital casualty, which are open 24 hours.

SERVICES BY DAY
DAY         SERVICE                                         WHO
Monday      Immunisation, child health, family planning     Nurses
Tuesday     Doctor consultations, chronic care review       Doctor + nurses
Wednesday   Antenatal care (morning), HIV testing           Nurses
Thursday    Doctor consultations, minor procedures          Doctor + nurses
Friday      Chronic medicine collection, wound care         Nurses
Saturday    Immunisation, minor ailments                    Nurse on duty

Basic laboratory tests done at the clinic: blood glucose, HbA1c, haemoglobin,
urine dipstick, pregnancy test, rapid HIV and malaria tests. Other samples are sent
to the Namibia Institute of Pathology; results take 3-5 working days.

FEES
- Consultation: N$ 10 (free for children under 5, pregnant women and pensioners)
- Chronic medicine collection: free
- Immunisation: free

WHAT TO BRING
- Your clinic card or ID document
- The child's Road-to-Health booklet for immunisation visits
- All medicines you currently take, in their boxes
- Any letters or results from other hospitals

CHRONIC MEDICINE COLLECTION
Patients with stable high blood pressure or diabetes may collect medicine every
month on Fridays without seeing the doctor. A review with the doctor is needed every
6 months, or sooner if readings are not controlled.

COMPLAINTS AND COMPLIMENTS
Speak to the clinic clerk or write in the book at reception. The clinic manager
replies within 10 working days.
