EMERGENCY GUIDE - BURNS
Document ID: HCC-PUB-006
Date: 2026-02-01
========================================================================
Follows standard first-aid practice.

1. Stop the burning: move away from the heat source.
2. Cool the burn under cool (not ice-cold) running water for 20 minutes.
3. Remove rings, watches and clothing near the burn unless stuck to the skin.
4. Cover loosely with cling film or a clean plastic bag.
5. Do NOT apply ice, butter, oil, toothpaste or traditional remedies.
6. Do not burst blisters.

GO TO THE CLINIC OR HOSPITAL IF
- The burn is larger than the person's palm, is deep, or on the face, hands, feet,
  genitals or over a joint
- The person is a child, elderly, or the burn is from electricity or chemicals
